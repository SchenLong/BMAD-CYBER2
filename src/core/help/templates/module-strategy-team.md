# Strategy Team Module

Executive strategy, leadership, and organizational decision-making. 14 agents
and 16 workflows covering strategic planning, crisis response, M&A, and
corporate governance.

## Workflows

| Command | Description | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| `/strategic-planning-session` | Long-term strategic planning session | Annual or quarterly planning exercises |
| `/crisis-response-planning` | Crisis communication and response | Preparing for or responding to crises |
| `/competitive-warfare` | Maximum intensity competitive strategy | Competitive positioning and offense |
| `/strategic-decision-workshop` | Multi-advisor strategic decision analysis | High-stakes decisions needing many viewpoints |
| `/ma-due-diligence` | M&A evaluation and integration strategy | Evaluating acquisition or merger targets |
| `/board-presentation-prep` | Prepare compelling board presentations | Board meeting preparation |
| `/board-relations-management` | Board engagement and relationship mgmt | Ongoing board relationship management |
| `/corporate-political-game` | Navigate internal politics and power | Internal organizational dynamics |
| `/ethical-dilemma-resolution` | Multi-perspective ethical analysis | Complex ethical decisions |
| `/leadership-philosophy` | Develop personal leadership philosophy | Executive self-development |
| `/leadership-transition-planning` | Succession and leadership handover | Leadership changes and succession |
| `/performance-review-preparation` | Executive performance review prep | Preparing for performance evaluations |
| `/policy-development` | Internal policy with ethics review | Writing organizational policies |
| `/political-risk-assessment` | Political risk evaluation for decisions | Assessing geopolitical factors |
| `/stakeholder-negotiation-prep` | Critical negotiation preparation | Preparing for high-stakes negotiations |

Note: `/conflict-resolution` is shared with core. Use `/strategy:conflict-resolution`
to specifically invoke the strategy team version.

## Key Agents

- **the-master-strategist** -- Grand strategy and long-term thinking
- **political-strategist** -- Political dynamics and positioning
- **communications-director** -- Strategic communications
- **debate-coach** -- Argumentation and persuasion
- **ethics-advisor** -- Ethical frameworks and analysis
- **policy-analyst** -- Policy analysis and development
- **stakeholder-mediator** -- Multi-party mediation

## Quick Start

Start a strategic planning session:

    /strategic-planning-session

Prepare for a crisis:

    /crisis-response-planning

Evaluate a potential acquisition:

    /ma-due-diligence

For more details: `/bmad-help search strategy`
