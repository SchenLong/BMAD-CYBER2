# Legal Team Module

Multi-jurisdictional legal analysis and document drafting. 13 agents and
7 workflows covering contracts, corporate formation, disputes, and tax planning.

## Workflows

| Command | Description | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| `/contract-review` | Contract risk and obligation analysis | Reviewing inbound contracts and agreements |
| `/contract-drafting` | Jurisdiction-appropriate contract creation | Drafting new legal agreements |
| `/corporate-formation` | Multi-jurisdictional entity formation | Setting up new business entities |
| `/cross-border-matter` | Multi-jurisdictional legal coordination | Matters spanning multiple countries |
| `/dispute-strategy` | Dispute analysis and resolution strategy | Handling legal disputes or litigation |
| `/legal-matter-intake` | Legal case intake and routing | Triaging new legal matters |
| `/tax-planning` | Tax optimization and compliance planning | Tax structuring and compliance |

## Key Agents

- **counsel** -- General legal counsel
- **advocate** -- Litigation and dispute specialist
- **charter** -- Corporate governance and formation
- **covenant** -- Contract law specialist
- **europa** -- EU and international law
- **liberty** -- Constitutional and civil rights
- **deed** -- Property and real estate law
- **tribute** -- Tax law specialist
- **baltic** -- Baltic/Nordic jurisdictions

## Quick Start

Review a contract for risks and obligations:

    /contract-review

Set up a new corporate entity:

    /corporate-formation

Plan a multi-jurisdictional matter:

    /cross-border-matter

For more details: `/bmad-help search legal`
