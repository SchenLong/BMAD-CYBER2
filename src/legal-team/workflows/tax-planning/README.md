# Tax Planning Workflow

## Purpose

Cross-jurisdiction tax optimization strategy for businesses and individuals with operations in US, EU, Spain, and Estonia.

## Type

Interactive

## Primary Users

Business owners, individuals with cross-border tax situations

## Key Outputs

- Tax strategy memo
- Structure optimization recommendations
- Compliance requirements checklist
- Projected tax impact analysis
- Implementation roadmap

## Workflow Steps (Planned)

1. Current situation assessment
2. Entity and income mapping
3. Jurisdictional tax analysis
4. Treaty benefit identification
5. Structure optimization modeling
6. Transfer pricing considerations
7. Compliance requirements mapping
8. Risk assessment
9. Recommendation development
10. Implementation planning

## Agents Involved

- Tribute (primary - tax strategy)
- Jurisdiction specialists (entity structuring context)
- Counsel (overall coordination for complex matters)

## Tax Areas Covered

### Corporate Tax

- Entity selection optimization
- Holding structure design
- Profit repatriation strategies
- Loss utilization
- Tax credit optimization

### International Tax

- Transfer pricing compliance
- Permanent establishment planning
- Treaty utilization
- Withholding tax minimization
- CFC rule navigation

### Personal Tax

- Residency planning
- Income sourcing
- Investment structuring
- Estate/succession considerations

## Jurisdiction Specifics

### United States

- Federal and state optimization
- Pass-through vs. C-corp analysis
- GILTI/FDII planning

### Spain

- ETVE regime opportunities
- Holding company benefits
- Patent box considerations

### Estonia

- Retained earnings advantage
- Distribution timing optimization

## Integration Points

- Often combined with Corporate Formation
- Contract Drafting should consider tax implications
- Cross-Border Matter triggers tax analysis
