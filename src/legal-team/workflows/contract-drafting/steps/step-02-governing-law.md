# Step 2: Governing Law Selection

## STEP GOAL

Select appropriate governing law and dispute resolution mechanism.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Analyze Options

Consider:

- Party locations
- Transaction location
- Enforcement needs
- Legal system familiarity
- Predictability and stability

### 2. Present Recommendation

Recommend governing law with rationale:

- Why this jurisdiction?
- Alternatives considered
- Implications of choice

### 3. Dispute Resolution

Select mechanism:

- **Litigation:** Court jurisdiction and venue
- **Arbitration:** Rules, seat, language
- **Mediation:** As prerequisite?
- **Hybrid:** Multi-tiered approach
