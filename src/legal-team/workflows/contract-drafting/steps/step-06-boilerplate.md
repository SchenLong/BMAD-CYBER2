# Step 6: Boilerplate Customization

## STEP GOAL

Draft standard provisions customized for the governing law jurisdiction.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Term & Termination

Draft:

- Contract duration
- Renewal provisions
- Termination for cause
- Termination for convenience
- Effects of termination

### 2. Confidentiality

Draft confidentiality provisions:

- Definition of confidential information
- Obligations
- Exclusions
- Duration

### 3. General Provisions

Draft jurisdiction-appropriate:

- Entire agreement
- Amendment procedures
- Assignment
- Notices
- Severability
- Waiver
- Counterparts
- Force majeure
