# Step 3: Legal Analysis

## STEP GOAL

Identify potential legal claims, defenses, and applicable law.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Claim Identification

Based on facts, identify potential claims:

**Contract Claims:**

- Breach of contract
- Breach of warranty
- Rescission
- Reformation
- Specific performance

**Tort Claims:**

- Negligence
- Fraud/Misrepresentation
- Interference with contract
- Conversion
- Defamation

**Statutory Claims:**

- Consumer protection violations
- Employment law violations
- Unfair competition
- Industry-specific regulations

**Equitable Claims:**

- Unjust enrichment
- Promissory estoppel
- Constructive trust

### 2. Elements Analysis

For each potential claim, analyze elements:

```markdown
### Claim: [Name]

**Elements Required:**
1. [Element 1] - [Can we prove? Evidence?]
2. [Element 2] - [Can we prove? Evidence?]
3. [Element 3] - [Can we prove? Evidence?]
...

**Overall Assessment:** [Strong/Moderate/Weak]
```

### 3. Defense Identification

If defending, identify potential defenses:

**Procedural Defenses:**

- Statute of limitations
- Lack of jurisdiction
- Improper venue
- Failure to state claim

**Substantive Defenses:**

- No breach occurred
- Excuse/Justification
- Waiver/Estoppel
- Comparative/Contributory fault
- Mitigation failure

**Affirmative Defenses:**

- Accord and satisfaction
- Release
- Statute of frauds
- Illegality

### 4. Applicable Law Research

Identify governing law:

**Jurisdiction Specific:**

- Applicable statutes
- Key case law
- Procedural requirements
- Limitations periods

**Spain Specific (if applicable):**

- Código Civil provisions
- Ley de Enjuiciamiento Civil
- Relevant jurisprudence
- Plazos de prescripción
