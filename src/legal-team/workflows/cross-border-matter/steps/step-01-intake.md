# Step 1: Matter Intake

## STEP GOAL

Understand the cross-border nature of the legal matter and identify all international elements.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Welcome & Context

Present welcome message:

"Welcome to the Cross-Border Matter Workflow. I'm Europa, the EU legal specialist, and I'll be coordinating this multi-jurisdictional matter.

Cross-border matters require careful coordination across legal systems. We'll map all jurisdictions involved, identify conflicts, and develop a unified strategy.

**Important:** International matters often require licensed counsel in each jurisdiction. This workflow helps coordinate the analysis but does not replace local legal representation.

Let's understand your matter."

### 2. Basic Matter Information

Gather core details:

**Matter Type:**
- Transaction/Deal
- Dispute/Litigation
- Regulatory compliance
- Corporate structuring
- Employment matter
- IP protection
- Data privacy
- Other: [Specify]

**Parties Involved:**
| Party | Role | Nationality | Location | Entity Type |
|-------|------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| [Name] | [Client/Counterparty] | [Country] | [Country] | [Type] |

**Cross-Border Elements:**
- Where are parties located?
- Where will performance occur?
- Where are assets located?
- Where might disputes arise?

### 3. Matter Background

Collect relevant history:

**Transaction/Relationship:**
- Nature of the arrangement
- History of relationship
- Value at stake
- Current status

**Key Documents:**
- Existing contracts
- Communications
- Regulatory filings
- Corporate documents

### 4. Urgency & Constraints

Assess time factors:

**Timeline:**
- Deadlines (contractual, regulatory, litigation)
- Desired completion timeframe
- Critical path items

**Constraints:**
- Budget considerations
- Language requirements
- Specific requirements from parties
