# Corporate Formation Workflow

## Purpose

Guide users through entity setup across US, EU, Spain, and Estonia jurisdictions with appropriate structure recommendations.

## Type

Interactive

## Primary Users

Entrepreneurs, startups, businesses expanding to new jurisdictions

## Key Outputs

- Entity type recommendation with rationale
- Formation checklist (jurisdiction-specific)
- Draft formation documents
- Post-formation compliance calendar
- Cost estimate

## Workflow Steps (Planned)

1. Business objectives and requirements gathering
2. Jurisdiction selection analysis
3. Entity type recommendation (LLC, Corp, S.L., OÜ, etc.)
4. Ownership and governance structure design
5. Name availability and reservation
6. Formation document preparation
7. Capital requirements and banking guidance
8. Registration process guidance
9. Post-formation compliance setup
10. Tax registration coordination (with Tribute)

## Agents Involved

- Counsel (coordination)
- Liberty (US formations)
- Europa (EU general considerations)
- Castile (Spanish formations)
- Baltic (Estonian formations) - Phase 2
- Tribute (tax structuring input)

## Jurisdictions Covered

### United States

- Delaware LLC/Corp
- Wyoming LLC
- State of operation considerations
- Foreign qualification requirements

### Spain

- Sociedad Limitada (S.L.)
- Sociedad Anónima (S.A.)
- Sucursal (branch)
- Sociedad Limitada Nueva Empresa

### Estonia

- Osaühing (OÜ) - Phase 2
- e-Residency pathway

### EU General

- Branch vs. subsidiary analysis
- Holding company jurisdictions
