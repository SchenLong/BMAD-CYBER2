# Campaign Planner - Organization

**Comprehensive organizational intelligence campaign planning and execution**

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Classification** | Organization Investigation |
| **Duration** | 2-3 hours |
| **Steps** | 9 |
| **Primary Agent** | Vector (OSINT Lead) |
| **Supporting Agents** | Resolver, Echo, Shadow, Proxy, Probe, Atlas, Dossier |

## Purpose

Full intelligence campaign against organizational targets:

- Corporate due diligence
- Competitor intelligence
- Threat actor organization profiling
- M&A intelligence support

## Steps

1. **Target Definition** (Vector) - Organization validation, requirements
2. **Infrastructure Mapping** (Resolver) - Domain/network reconnaissance
3. **Technology Stack** (Probe) - Technology identification
4. **Social Presence** (Echo) - Corporate social footprint
5. **Dark Web Exposure** (Shadow) - Breach and leak assessment
6. **Corporate Structure** (Proxy) - Entity relationships, ownership
7. **Geospatial Analysis** (Atlas) - Locations, facilities
8. **Threat Assessment** (Dossier) - Risk and threat correlation
9. **Campaign Assembly** (Vector) - Final intelligence package

## Outputs

- Organizational intelligence package
- Corporate structure map
- Technical infrastructure assessment
- Risk and exposure report

```
/intel-team:osint-lead
> Campaign Planner - Organization
```
