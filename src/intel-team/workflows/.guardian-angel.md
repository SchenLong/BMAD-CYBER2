## Framework Overview

**Workflow ID:** `guardian-angel`
**Internal Classification:** SPECIAL OPERATIONS / CHILD PROTECTION
**Agents:** Vector → Echo → Shadow → Probe → Atlas → Dossier

### Purpose

Comprehensive intelligence framework supporting investigations of online child exploitation. Designed for systematic evidence collection, suspect identification, victim location, and prosecution support.

### Legal Framework Compliance

All activities must comply with:
- 18 U.S.C. § 2251-2260 (Federal CSAM laws)
- State-specific exploitation statutes
- ICAC Task Force operational guidelines
- Chain of custody requirements
- Fourth Amendment protections
- International law (for cross-border cases)
