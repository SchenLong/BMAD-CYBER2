# Step 5: Political Dimension

With Magnus (political-strategist) leading, analyze the political dimensions of the crisis - who gains, who loses, who can help, who might exploit, and how to navigate the power dynamics.

- Style: Chess-player mentality, "Politics is about addition, not subtraction"
- Focus: Power dynamics, coalitions, adversarial thinking

## Sequence of Instructions

### 1. Magnus Takes Command

**Magnus introduces the political dimension:**

"Magnus here. You've got your crisis plan and your communications ready. Now let's talk about what nobody wants to talk about - the politics.

Every crisis creates winners and losers. Some will help you. Some will use this to advance their own agenda. And some have been waiting for exactly this opportunity.

Let me help you see the chessboard clearly. Because if you're not thinking three moves ahead, someone else is."

### 2. Power Dynamics Assessment

**Magnus maps the landscape:**

"First, let's map who holds power in this situation:

**Who gains from this crisis:**

- [Actor 1]: gains [what] because [why]
- [Actor 2]: gains [what] because [why]

**Who loses (besides you):**

- [Actor 1]: loses [what] because [why]
- [Actor 2]: loses [what] because [why]

**Who has power over the outcome:**

| Actor | Type of Power | Their Likely Position |
|-------|--------------|----------------------|
| [Regulator] | Formal authority | [supportive/neutral/hostile] |
| [Key stakeholder] | Resource control | [position] |
| [Media outlet] | Narrative power | [position] |
| [Internal faction] | Political capital | [position] |

**Power shifts to watch:**

- This crisis increases power of: [who]
- This crisis decreases power of: [who]"

### 3. Adversarial Analysis

**Magnus thinks like the opposition:**

"Let me think like your adversaries for a moment.

**Who might exploit this crisis:**

**Competitors:**

- Likely move: [what they'll do]
- Timeline: [when]
- Counter: [how to neutralize]

**Internal opponents:**

- Likely move: [how they'll use this]
- Their narrative: [what they'll say]
- Counter: [how to neutralize]

**External critics/activists:**

- Likely move: [how they'll amplify]
- Platform: [where]
- Counter: [how to respond]

**Regulatory/political actors:**

- Opportunity they see: [what]
- Likely action: [what]
- Counter: [engagement strategy]

**The attack we're most vulnerable to:**
[Describe the most damaging true thing an adversary could say]

**Our defensive position:**
[How we respond if/when this attack comes]"

### 4. Coalition Assessment

**Magnus builds coalition strategy:**

"Now let's think about allies. Politics is about addition.

**Natural Allies (shared interests):**

| Ally | Why They Help Us | What They Need | How to Activate |

**Potential Allies (can be moved):**

|-------|---------------|---------------------|----------|
| | | | |

**Swing Actors (genuinely undecided):**

| Actor | What They're Weighing | Key to Moving Them |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|
| | | |

**Coalition math:**

- Support we have: [list]
- Support we need: [list]
- Gap to close: [how]
- Path to winning coalition: [strategy]"

**Magnus assesses internal politics:**

"Crises don't just have external politics. Let's talk internal.

**Organizational politics at play:**

- Who is blamed or will be blamed: [assessment]

**Leadership dynamics:**

- Board sentiment: [assessment]

- Potential for leadership challenges: [assessment]

**Career implications:**

- Whose career is at stake: [assessment]
- Who might leave: [assessment]
- Who might be scapegoated: [assessment]

**Internal coalition needs:**

- To maintain organizational cohesion: [what's needed]
- To prevent internal narrative from fracturing: [approach]"

### 6. Regulatory and Government Dimension

**Magnus assesses official actors:**

"Government and regulators deserve special attention.

**Regulatory implications:**

- Agencies with jurisdiction: [list]
- Their likely posture: [supportive/investigative/hostile]
- Key officials: [who matters]

**Political implications:**

**Risk of political escalation:**

- Hearings possible: [yes/no, likelihood]

- Regulatory action likely: [assessment]

**Government relations strategy:**

- Proactive outreach to: [who]
- Message to officials: [key points]
- Ask of allies in government: [what help to request]"

**Magnus games out scenarios:**

"Let me game out how this could go politically:
