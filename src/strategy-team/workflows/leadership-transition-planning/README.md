# Leadership Transition Planning Workflow

## Overview

A comprehensive 7-step workflow for planning and executing leadership transitions, including CEO succession, executive departures, and leadership restructuring. Ensures smooth handovers that preserve institutional knowledge and maintain organizational momentum.

## Workflow Structure

```

├── workflow.md

    ├── step-01-init.md

    ├── step-02-successor-assessment.md
    ├── step-03-knowledge-transfer.md
    ├── step-04-stakeholder-management.md
    ├── step-05-operational-continuity.md
    ├── step-06-transition-timeline.md
    └── step-07-transition-document.md
```

## Coverage

- **Transition Framing**: Type, urgency, timeline, success criteria
- **Successor Assessment**: Candidate evaluation, selection process, development needs
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Critical knowledge, relationships, tacit expertise
- **Stakeholder Management**: Communications, relationship transitions
- **Operational Continuity**: Business continuity, risk mitigation
- **Transition Timeline**: Detailed handover schedule, milestones
- **Transition Document**: Board-ready succession plan

## Transition Types Covered

- **Planned succession**: Retirement, term completion
- **Unexpected departure**: Resignation, termination, health
- **Organizational restructuring**: Role elimination, merger integration
- **Interim leadership**: Temporary coverage, search process
- **Development transition**: Promotion, role expansion
