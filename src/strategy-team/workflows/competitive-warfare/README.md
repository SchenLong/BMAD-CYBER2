# Competitive Warfare Workflow

## Overview

A strategic 9-step workflow for planning and executing competitive warfare strategies. Draws on military strategy principles adapted for business contexts, helping executives navigate high-stakes competitive battles.

## Strategic Frameworks

- Sun Tzu's Art of War principles
- Porter's Competitive Strategy
- OODA Loop (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act)
- Competitive intelligence methodology

## Coverage

- **Intelligence Gathering**: Competitor analysis, market dynamics
- **Terrain Analysis**: Market landscape, regulatory environment
- **Force Assessment**: Strengths, weaknesses, capabilities
- **Strategy Selection**: Attack, defense, flanking, niche
- **Tactical Planning**: Specific actions, timing, resources
- **Contingency Planning**: Response scenarios, pivot strategies

## Related Agents

- **Sun** (Master Strategist): Supreme strategic guidance
- **Musashi** (Strategist-Warrior): Timing and decisive action
- **Niccolo** (The Realist): Pragmatic power assessment
- **Magnus** (Political Strategist): Campaign tactics
