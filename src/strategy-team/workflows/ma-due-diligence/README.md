# M&A Due Diligence Workflow

## Overview

A comprehensive 8-step workflow for merger and acquisition due diligence, leveraging strategic advisors to evaluate targets, assess risks, and develop integration strategies. Ideal for acquisitions, mergers, strategic investments, and joint ventures.

## Coverage

- **Deal Thesis**: Strategic rationale, target criteria, success metrics
- **Strategic Fit**: Market position, competitive dynamics, strategic alignment
- **Financial Assessment**: Valuation, synergies, capital structure
- **Operational Diligence**: Operations, technology, talent, culture
- **Risk Identification**: Deal risks, integration risks, market risks
- **Integration Planning**: Day 1 readiness, 100-day plan, synergy capture
- **Stakeholder Communications**: Internal and external messaging strategy
- **Deal Recommendation**: Board-ready synthesis and recommendation
