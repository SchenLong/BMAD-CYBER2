Verify internal consistency, definitions usage, and cross-references.

- No circular definitions

### 2. Cross-Reference Verification

- Exhibit/Schedule references
- "Herein", "hereof" usage
- No orphaned references

### 3. Consistency Check

Verify:

- Date consistency
