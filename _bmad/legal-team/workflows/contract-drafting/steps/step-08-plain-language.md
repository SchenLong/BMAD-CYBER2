# Step 8: Plain Language Review

## STEP GOAL

Review contract for clarity, readability, and plain language.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Clarity Review

Check for:
- Unnecessarily complex sentences
- Ambiguous language
- Legalese that can be simplified
- Double negatives

### 2. Readability Assessment

Review:
- Sentence length
- Paragraph structure
- Logical flow
- Active vs. passive voice

### 3. Simplification

Where appropriate:
- Simplify complex provisions
- Add clarifying language
- Improve structure

Note: Maintain legal precision while improving readability.
