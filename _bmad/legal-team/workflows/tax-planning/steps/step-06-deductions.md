# Step 6: Deduction Maximization

## STEP GOAL

Identify and maximize available deductions and tax credits.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Business Deductions Review

Catalog deductible expenses:

**Operating Expenses:**
- Salaries and wages
- Rent and occupancy costs
- Professional services
- Marketing and advertising
- Travel and entertainment
- Supplies and materials

**Depreciation & Amortization:**
- Tangible property depreciation
- Intangible amortization
- Section 179 expensing
- Bonus depreciation
- Cost segregation opportunities

**Interest Expense:**
- Business interest limitation (163(j))
- Investment interest
- Mortgage interest
- Related party interest rules

### 2. Deduction Acceleration Strategies

Maximize current-year deductions:

**Timing Strategies:**
- Prepay deductible expenses
- Accelerate purchases before year-end
- Bonus depreciation elections
- Accounting method changes

**Cost Segregation:**
- Building purchase analysis
- Component breakdown
- 5/7/15 year property identification
- QIP (Qualified Improvement Property)

### 3. Tax Credit Identification

Identify available credits:

**USA Federal Credits:**
| Credit | Description | Amount | Criteria |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| R&D Credit | Research activities | 20% or simplified | Qualified research |
| Work Opportunity | Hiring incentives | Up to $9,600 | Target groups |
| Energy Credits | Clean energy | Varies | Qualifying property |
| Foreign Tax Credit | Avoid double tax | Actual foreign tax | Foreign source income |

**Spain Credits:**
| Credit | Description | Amount | Criteria |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| R&D Credit | I+D+i | 25%-42% | Qualifying activities |
| Job Creation | Employment | Varies | Net new employment |
| Film Production | Audiovisual | 30%/25% | Qualifying production |
| Patent Box | IP exploitation | Effective 10% | Qualifying IP |

### 4. Retirement & Benefit Plans

Maximize tax-advantaged savings:

**Retirement Vehicles:**
| Plan Type | Limit | Tax Treatment | Best For |
|-----------|-------|---------------|----------|
| 401(k) | $23,000 + catch-up | Pre-tax | Employees |
| SEP-IRA | Up to $69,000 | Pre-tax | Self-employed |
| Defined Benefit | Based on actuarial | Pre-tax | High earners |
| Cash Balance | Varies | Pre-tax | Professionals |

**Spain Options:**
- Plan de Pensiones
- EPSV (Basque Country)
- Plan de Previsión Asegurado
