# Step 5: Risk Allocation Assessment

## STEP GOAL

Analyze representations, warranties, indemnification, and limitation of liability.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Representations & Warranties

For each R&W:
- Who makes it?
- Scope and breadth
- Survival period
- Any caps or baskets

### 2. Indemnification

Review:
- Indemnification triggers
- Scope of indemnity
- Procedural requirements
- Caps and limitations
- Carve-outs

### 3. Limitation of Liability

Assess:
- Liability cap amount/formula
- Consequential damages exclusion
- Exclusions from cap
- Enforceability concerns

### 4. Insurance

Review:
- Insurance requirements
- Coverage types and amounts
- Certificate requirements
- Named insured provisions
