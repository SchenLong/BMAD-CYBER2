# Step 8: Recommendations Compilation

## STEP GOAL

Compile all findings into prioritized recommendations for contract modification.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Consolidate Findings

Gather all issues identified:
- Structure issues (Step 3)
- Substantive concerns (Step 4)
- Risk allocation issues (Step 5)
- Compliance requirements (Step 6)
- Gaps identified (Step 7)

### 2. Prioritize Recommendations

Categorize by priority:

**HIGH (Critical):**
- Legal compliance issues
- Significant liability exposure
- Unenforceable provisions
- Missing essential terms

**MEDIUM (Important):**
- Risk allocation imbalances
- Ambiguous terms
- Missing standard protections
- Negotiation leverage points

**LOW (Nice to Have):**
- Style/drafting improvements
- Minor clarifications
- Optional protections
- Best practice enhancements

### 3. Negotiation Strategy

For each recommendation:
- Suggested language
- Negotiation rationale
- Fall-back positions
- Deal-breaker assessment

```markdown

   - Risk: [exposure]
   - Recommendation: [suggested change]
```
