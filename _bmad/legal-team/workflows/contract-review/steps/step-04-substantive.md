# Step 4: Substantive Terms Analysis

## STEP GOAL

Review the core obligations, performance standards, and commercial terms.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Party Obligations

For each party, identify:
- Core obligations
- Performance standards
- Deliverables
- Timelines

### 2. Payment Terms

Review:
- Payment amounts and structure
- Payment timing
- Late payment consequences
- Currency and method

### 3. Performance Standards

Assess:
- Are obligations clearly defined?
- Are performance metrics measurable?
- Acceptance criteria present?
- Service levels (if applicable)?

### 4. Term and Renewal

Review:
- Contract duration
- Renewal provisions
- Auto-renewal concerns
- Early termination options
