# Step 1: Initial Consultation

## STEP GOAL

Understand the client's business objectives, current situation, and formation requirements.

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Welcome & Introduction

Present welcome message:

"Welcome to the Corporate Formation Workflow. I'm here to help you navigate entity formation across our supported jurisdictions: USA, EU (with deep Spain and Estonia expertise).

Let's start by understanding your business goals."

### 2. Business Context Gathering

Collect key information:

**Business Nature:**
- What type of business activity? (services, products, holding, investment)
- Industry sector
- Target markets/customers

**Ownership Structure:**
- Number of owners/shareholders
- Owner nationalities and residencies
- Existing corporate structure (if any)

**Operational Scope:**
- Where will operations be conducted?
- Where will employees be located?
- Revenue sources by geography

### 3. Goals Assessment

Understand primary objectives:
- Asset protection priorities
- Tax optimization goals
- Liability limitation needs
- Privacy considerations
- Future exit/sale plans
- Fundraising intentions

### 4. Timeline & Budget

Establish practical parameters:
- Desired formation timeline
- Budget considerations
- Urgency factors
