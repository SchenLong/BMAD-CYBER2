# Step 8: Matter Brief Generation & Next Steps

## STEP GOAL

Generate a comprehensive matter brief document and provide clear next steps guidance.

## EXECUTION RULES

- Compile all gathered information into final brief
- Provide clear, actionable next steps
- Include legal disclaimer
- Offer to hand off to recommended specialist
- ALWAYS communicate in `{communication_language}`

## EXECUTION SEQUENCE

### 1. Compile Matter Brief

Generate a comprehensive summary of:
- Initial context and situation overview
- Matter classification
- Jurisdiction analysis
- Urgency assessment
- Party analysis
- Document status
- Routing recommendation

### 2. Finalize Output Document

Complete the matter brief with:

```markdown
## 8. Next Steps & Recommendations

### Immediate Actions
1. [Action item with timeline]
2. [Action item with timeline]
3. [Action item with timeline]

### Recommended Workflow Execution
- **Workflow:** [recommended workflow]
- **Specialist:** [primary agent]
- **Expected Outcome:** [what user will receive]

### Documents to Prepare
[List any documents user should gather]

### Questions to Consider
[Key questions that will be addressed]

---

## Executive Summary

**Matter:** [brief description]
**Classification:** [matter type]
**Jurisdiction:** [primary jurisdiction]
**Urgency:** [level]
**Recommended Action:** [summary recommendation]

---

## Legal Disclaimer

**DISCLAIMER:** This analysis is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. The Legal Team module provides general legal information and guidance but is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified attorney. No attorney-client relationship is created through use of this module. For specific legal matters, please consult with a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.

---

*Generated by Legal Team Module - Matter Intake Workflow*
*Date: {date}*
*Intake conducted by: Counsel (General Counsel)*
```

Update frontmatter:
- `stepsCompleted: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8]`
- `status: "completed"`

### 3. Present Next Steps

"Your matter brief is complete. Here's what I recommend for next steps..."

Present:
- Summary of key findings
- Recommended specialist to engage
- Suggested workflow to execute
- Any immediate actions needed

### 4. Offer Handoff

"Would you like me to connect you with [recommended specialist] now to continue with [recommended workflow]?"

### 5. Present Final Menu

**Select an Option:**
- **[S]** Start recommended workflow with specialist
- **[P]** Party Mode - discuss with multiple specialists
- **[D]** Download/save matter brief
- **[E]** Exit intake process

#### Menu Handling:
- IF S: Load recommended specialist agent and workflow
- IF P: Execute party-mode workflow with legal team context
- IF D: Confirm file saved and provide location
- IF E: Thank user and conclude session

---

## WORKFLOW COMPLETION

When user selects an exit option:

"Thank you for using the Legal Team Matter Intake. Your matter brief has been saved to:
`{output_folder}/legal/matter-brief-{matter_name}.md`

[If routing to specialist]: I'm now connecting you with [Agent Name] to continue with your [matter type] matter.

[If exiting]: Feel free to return anytime you need legal guidance. Good luck with your matter!"
