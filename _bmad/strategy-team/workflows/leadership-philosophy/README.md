# Leadership Philosophy Development Workflow

## Overview

A 7-step workflow for developing a personal leadership philosophy through dialogue with historical archetypes and deep reflection. Helps executives articulate their core values, leadership style, and guiding principles.

## Workflow Structure

```
leadership-philosophy/
├── workflow.md
├── README.md
└── steps/
    ├── step-01-init.md
    ├── step-01b-continue.md
    ├── step-02-self-assessment.md
    ├── step-03-archetype-dialogue.md
    ├── step-04-values-clarification.md
    ├── step-05-philosophy-synthesis.md
    ├── step-06-practical-application.md
    └── step-07-documentation.md
```

## Coverage

- **Self-Assessment**: Leadership experiences, strengths, growth areas
- **Archetype Dialogue**: Conversations with historical leaders
- **Values Clarification**: Core beliefs, non-negotiables, priorities
- **Philosophy Synthesis**: Personal leadership statement
- **Practical Application**: Day-to-day leadership behaviors
- **Documentation**: Written philosophy, sharing with team

## Historical Archetypes Consulted

- **Niccolo** (The Realist): Pragmatic power and results
- **Charles** (The Liberator): Moral courage and transformation
- **Maximilien** (The Revolutionary): Change and conviction
- **Burke** (The Conservative): Tradition and stability
- **Lee** (The Technocrat): Systems and efficiency
- **Musashi** (The Warrior): Discipline and decisiveness
- **Sun** (The Strategist): Wisdom and foresight
- **Jean-Luc** (The Commander): Principles and diplomacy

## Related Agents

- All historical archetype agents participate in dialogue
- **Sophia** (Ethics Advisor): Values alignment
- **Cicero** (Debate Coach): Articulation and clarity
