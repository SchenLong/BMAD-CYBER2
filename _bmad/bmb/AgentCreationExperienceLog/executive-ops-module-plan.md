## 1. Module Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The **Executive Operations Module** provides AI-powered advisory capabilities for executives, leaders, and decision-makers. It introduces political acumen, strategic decision-making, project governance, and organizational leadership frameworks to help leaders navigate complex organizational dynamics.

### 1.2 Target Users

- **C-Suite Executives** (CEO, COO, CFO, CTO, CISO)
- **Board Members & Directors**
- **VP-Level Leadership**
- **Program/Portfolio Managers**
- **Political Leaders & Advisors**
- **Organizational Change Agents**
- **Strategy Consultants**

### 1.3 Core Value Proposition

Transform AI into a trusted executive advisor that:
- Provides strategic counsel on high-stakes decisions
- Navigates organizational politics and stakeholder dynamics
- Structures complex projects and programs for success
- Offers diverse leadership perspectives (operational, political, visionary)
- Facilitates board-level governance and oversight
- Guides organizational transformation and change management

### 1.4 Key Differentiators from General AI

1. **Political Intelligence** - Understanding of power dynamics, influence, and stakeholder management
2. **Decision Frameworks** - Structured approaches to high-stakes decisions (not just analysis)
3. **Leadership Personas** - Distinct advisory voices (pragmatist, strategist, politician, visionary)
4. **Executive Communication** - Board-ready deliverables and executive summaries
5. **Project Governance** - Beyond PM fundamentals to portfolio governance and oversight
6. **Change Leadership** - Organizational transformation, not just project management

## 2. Domain Analysis

### 2.1 Frameworks & Methodologies to Implement

#### Strategic Decision-Making
- **RAPID Decision Framework** (Recommend, Agree, Perform, Input, Decide)
- **OODA Loop** (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) - Military-derived decision cycle
- **Cynefin Framework** (Simple, Complicated, Complex, Chaotic domains)
- **Pre-Mortem Analysis** (Prospective hindsight for risk identification)
- **Red Team / Blue Team** decision challenge
- **Vroom-Yetton Decision Model** (Leadership & participation)

#### Political & Stakeholder Management
- **Stakeholder Power-Interest Matrix**
- **Influence Without Authority** strategies
- **Coalition Building** frameworks
- **Political Capital** management
- **BATNA/WATNA** (Negotiation theory)
- **Win-Win Negotiation** (Principled negotiation)

#### Project & Portfolio Governance
- **PMBOK / PMI Standards** (Project Management Institute)
- **PRINCE2** governance model
- **SAFe Portfolio Management** (Scaled Agile)
- **Stage-Gate Process** (project go/no-go decisions)
- **Benefits Realization Management**
- **OKR/KPI Governance**

#### Organizational Change
- **Kotter's 8-Step Change Model**
- **ADKAR** (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement)
- **McKinsey 7-S Framework**
- **Lewin's Change Model** (Unfreeze, Change, Refreeze)
- **Organizational Readiness Assessment**

#### Leadership Models
- **Situational Leadership** (Hersey-Blanchard)
- **Transformational vs. Transactional Leadership**
- **Servant Leadership**
- **Level 5 Leadership** (Collins)
- **Executive Presence** frameworks

## 4. Proposed Workflows (5-7 Workflows)

### 4.1 Strategic Decision Workshop
**ID:** strategic-decision-workshop
**Type:** Guided Process (6-8 steps)
**Duration:** 2-4 hours
**Output:** Strategic Decision Brief (board-ready document)

**Purpose:** Guide executives through high-stakes decisions using multiple frameworks

**Steps:**
1. Decision Context & Stakes Definition
2. Stakeholder Analysis (who decides, who influences, who's impacted)
3. Options Development (divergent thinking)
4. Framework Selection (RAPID, Cynefin, Vroom-Yetton based on decision type)
5. Options Evaluation (Red Team challenge)
6. Pre-Mortem Analysis (what could go wrong)
7. Decision Documentation & Communication Plan
8. Implementation Planning

**Frameworks Used:** RAPID, Cynefin, Pre-Mortem, Red Team

---

### 4.2 Stakeholder Influence Campaign
**ID:** stakeholder-influence-campaign
**Type:** Planning Process (5-6 steps)
**Duration:** 1-3 hours
**Output:** Influence Strategy Document

**Purpose:** Plan and execute influence strategies for critical initiatives

**Steps:**
1. Initiative Definition & Success Criteria
2. Stakeholder Mapping (Power-Interest Matrix)
3. Current State Assessment (support/oppose/neutral)
4. Influence Strategy Development (per stakeholder)
5. Coalition Building Plan
6. Execution Timeline & Checkpoints

**Frameworks Used:** Power-Interest Matrix, Influence Without Authority, Coalition Building

---

### 4.3 Program Governance Setup
**ID:** program-governance-setup
**Type:** Configuration Process (7-8 steps)
**Duration:** 3-5 hours
**Output:** Program Governance Charter

**Purpose:** Establish governance framework for major programs or portfolios

**Steps:**
1. Program/Portfolio Definition
2. Governance Model Selection (PRINCE2, PMI, Custom)
3. Decision Authority Framework (RAPID/RACI)
4. Stage-Gate Criteria Definition
5. Reporting & Escalation Framework
6. Risk Governance Model
7. Benefits Realization Framework
8. Change Control Process

**Frameworks Used:** PRINCE2, PMBOK, Stage-Gate, Benefits Realization

---

### 4.4 Organizational Change Blueprint
**ID:** organizational-change-blueprint
**Type:** Planning Process (8-10 steps)
**Duration:** 4-6 hours
**Output:** Change Management Plan

**Purpose:** Design comprehensive change management approach for transformations

**Steps:**
1. Change Definition & Scope
2. Organizational Readiness Assessment
3. Stakeholder Impact Analysis

5. Resistance Anticipation & Mitigation
6. Communication Strategy

**Frameworks Used:** Kotter 8-Step, ADKAR, McKinsey 7-S

## 8. Risk Assessment

### Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Agents too generic/consultant-speak | Medium | High | Detailed personas with specific backgrounds, signature phrases |
| Political advice too Machiavellian | Medium | Medium | Include ethical guardrails in persona principles |
| Overlap with general business advice | High | Medium | Focus on executive-level, high-stakes scenarios |
| Workflows too complex for single session | Medium | Medium | Design for multi-session continuation |
| Framework overload | Low | Medium | Agents should suggest frameworks, not force them |
