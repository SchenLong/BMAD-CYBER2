## Overview

The module consists of **two categories** of agents:

### Category A: Modern Professional Advisors (6 agents)
Contemporary experts with real-world professional backgrounds

### Category B: Historical Archetype Advisors (6 agents)
Philosophical archetypes channeling historical figures to provide diverse ideological perspectives

   - Pros: Parallel to cyber-ops, professional
   - Cons: Doesn't capture political/strategic nature

2. **strategic-ops** (Strategic Operations)
   - Pros: Captures strategic thinking
   - Cons: Generic

3. **leadership-ops** (Leadership Operations)
   - Pros: Executive-focused
   - Cons: Broad

4. **counsel-ops** (Counsel Operations)
   - Pros: Advisory nature clear
   - Cons: Legal connotation

5. **decision-ops** (Decision Operations)
   - Pros: Decision-focused
   - Cons: Generic

**Recommendation:** Keep **exec-ops** for parallel with cyber-ops
