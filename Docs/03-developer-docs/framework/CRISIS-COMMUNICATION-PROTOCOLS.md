## 🔒 Operational Security (OPSEC) During Crisis

### Information Classification During Crisis

#### Public Information (Cleared for external communication)
- General incident response capability
- Professional response activation confirmation
- Timeline for regular updates
- Contact information for coordination

#### Restricted Information (Internal stakeholders only)
- Specific attack vectors or methodologies
- Detailed technical findings or vulnerabilities
- Internal system information or configurations
- Response tactics, techniques, and procedures

#### Classified Information (Command team only)
- Adversary attribution or intelligence
- Law enforcement coordination details
- Sensitive customer or business information
- Advanced response capabilities or tools

### OPSEC Communication Rules

1. **Need-to-Know Basis**: Information shared only with stakeholders requiring it for their role
2. **Minimum Necessary**: Provide sufficient information for decision-making, no more
3. **Verified Channels**: Use secure, authenticated communication channels only
4. **Documentation Control**: Maintain control of all written communications and recordings
5. **External Coordination**: Clear all external communications through command structure
