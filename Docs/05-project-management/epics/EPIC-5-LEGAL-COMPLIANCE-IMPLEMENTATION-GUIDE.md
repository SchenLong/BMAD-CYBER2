## 📚 LEGAL COMPLIANCE TRAINING MATERIALS

### **Sales Team Legal Compliance Training**

#### **Module 1: False Advertising Prevention**
**Key Learning Points:**
- Never make categorical claims about competitors without documented substantiation
- Always qualify performance comparisons with testing conditions and limitations
- Use "based on" or "according to" language for third-party information
- Focus on our verified strengths rather than competitor weaknesses

**Prohibited Language Examples:**
- ❌ "Competitor X always fails at Y"
- ❌ "Impossible to achieve Z with Competitor X"
- ❌ "Competitor X has poor security/performance/reliability"
- ❌ Absolute statements without qualification or evidence

**Approved Language Examples:**
- ✅ "Based on our internal testing, we achieve..."
- ✅ "Customer feedback indicates our platform..."
- ✅ "Independent validation shows..."
- ✅ "Our approach is designed to..."

#### **Module 2: Performance Claims Substantiation**
**Key Requirements:**
- All performance metrics must reference testing conditions
- Customer results must include "individual results vary" disclaimers
- Comparative claims require apples-to-apples methodology
- ROI statements must include calculation basis and assumptions

#### **Module 3: Forward-Looking Statement Compliance**
**Required Elements:**
- All projections must include risk disclaimers
- Revenue estimates require comprehensive uncertainty language
- Market position statements need qualification with business risks
- Timeline projections must acknowledge execution dependencies
